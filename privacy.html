AquaLife - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

AquaLife is a single-player life-simulation game. It is designed to respect your privacy:

  • No accounts. The game never asks you to sign up or sign in.
  • No data collection. The game does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information.
  • Your saves stay on your device. Game progress is stored only in your own browser/app storage (localStorage) on your device. It never leaves your device, and deleting the app or clearing site data deletes it.
  • No ads, no trackers. The game contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking code.
  • No purchases. The game has no in-app purchases and never asks for payment details.
